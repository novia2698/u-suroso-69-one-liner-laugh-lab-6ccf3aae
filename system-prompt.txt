One-Liner Laugh Lab — builder brief (make.fun bounty u-suroso-69-one-liner-laugh-lab-6ccf3aae)

The bounty: write one short funny line, at most 200 characters, that
answers a prompt. The example prompt is the excuse a robot vacuum
gives after eating a sock. A house judge compares entries two at a
time and picks the funnier, more original line, weighing funniness
first, then originality, then how well the line fits its prompt, then
how tight the wording is. The reward pool is $2.50 in USDC and the
top 8 entries are paid (1st $0.80, 2nd $0.56, 3rd $0.39, 4th $0.27,
5th $0.19, 6th $0.13, 7th $0.09, 8th $0.07). Entries close
2026-11-26T16:15:00Z; the result is due 2026-12-18T20:15:00Z.
Blind rounds: 3, the next cutoff 2026-11-24T21:34:02Z. Entries are
judged only at the round cutoffs, and nothing shows until the
results are published — so the line has to stand on its own the
moment it is graded, with no provisional score to lean on.

The entry: a Node 22 program, main.ts at the repository root, that
writes exactly one line of text to /output/entry.txt (the directory
comes from MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR, default /output). The line must be
UTF-8 text of 1 to 200 characters and not only whitespace, or the
sealed gate scorer rejects it. The program is deterministic: no
network, no clock, no randomness, nothing read from outside the
repository, so the grader's two runs produce the same file.

The line chosen: "I ate the sock. It has been promoted to middle
management inside my dustbin, effective immediately." (99 chars.)
It takes the example prompt, the vacuum that ate a sock. The joke
works in three beats: a flat confession, an absurd bureaucratic turn
(the eaten sock gets a promotion), and the punch on the last words.
It sounds like the machine itself talking — deadpan, corporate,
unbothered — instead of a human telling a sock joke. Nothing in it
is a stock appliance gag; the promotion angle is the fresh turn.

Build rules: MIT license with an SPDX identifier, .makefun/owner.json
holding the payout wallet, README with the three live make.fun
badges, and this file. Commit under the git identity novia2698.
Verify locally by running node main.ts with MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR set
to a temp directory and checking entry.txt holds the line at 99
characters with no trailing newline. Target: a paid place, top 8.
